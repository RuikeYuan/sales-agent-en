# Training Salon Rules v1

These rules apply only to the fictional cases in this package. They are not real salon policies, medical advice, or legal conclusions. See [source distillation](source-distillation.md) for provenance.

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Service | Non-invasive basic cleansing and moisturizing care; no injections, lasers, or treatment procedures | 01, 05 |
| First-visit trial | CNY 79 for one visit, approximately 35 minutes; verify first-visit eligibility | 01, 05 |
| Regular single visit | CNY 240, approximately 40 minutes | 01, 03 |
| Four-visit package | CNY 880, valid for four months, CNY 220 deducted per visit | 01–05 |
| Inclusions | All three prices cover the same basic service; specific products are listed on the service form | 01, 05 |
| Refunds for unused visits | A refund can be requested at CNY 220 per unused visit; used visits are not repriced at CNY 240. Disputes over used services require separate review | 02, 04 |
| Separate orders | The completed CNY 79 trial is separate and uses no visits from a new package; buying a package requires a new payment of CNY 880 | 04 |
| Discounts | No two-visit package and no retroactive conversion to a package; do not invent discounts or gifts on the spot | 02, 03, 04 |
| Other terms | No mandatory products, automatic renewal, or cross-location use | 02, 03, 04 |
| Photos | Optional, no extra charge; written records alone are allowed. Records are neither a diagnosis nor proof of results | 01, 02, 04 |

The material does not specify the following; mark them as requiring verification: actual appointment availability, exact rescheduling terms, refund processing time, treatment of unused visits after expiry, when the validity period starts, specific products/ingredients, and holiday hours. Do not turn "a refund can be requested" into "instant refunds at any time" or "unconditional refunds even after expiry."

Budget comparisons neither recommend visit frequency nor lock in future prices. All regular single visits: n × 240. If n is a multiple of 4, all package visits: (n ÷ 4) × 880. Otherwise, explicitly use a combination of full packages and remaining single visits. Each package's validity and the customer's ability to attend require separate consideration.
