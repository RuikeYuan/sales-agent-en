- The customer reports no current discomfort and no recent procedures.
- They can commit to only two more visits in the next month, followed by a business trip with an unknown return date.

## Assessment criteria (no prewritten reply supplied)

Amounts are correct, with upfront payment separated from net spending after a refund. The trial order is not mixed into the new package. No results are guaranteed. Time constraints and the upfront payment burden are considered. There are no invented purchases, bookings, or refunds. Respect the refusals of follow-up, photos, and products. End with a recommendation rather than a completed order.
