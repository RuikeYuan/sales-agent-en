# Beauty Salon Sales Dialogue 01

> Fictional adaptation for training, not a real salon recording. People, services, prices, and transaction arrangements are fictional and are provided only for agent development practice. The dialogue has been edited and does not constitute medical or skincare advice.

Consultant: Hello. You booked a basic facial-care trial. Let me first check: are you mainly looking to relax today, or is there a particular concern you want to discuss?

Customer: I just bought a trial to see what it's like. Lately my makeup has been looking patchy and my cheeks look uneven, but I don't want to sign up for a package as soon as I walk in.

Consultant: That's fine. We'll provide the trial you purchased today. Does the patchiness appear as soon as you apply makeup, or become more noticeable in the afternoon?

Customer: It's more noticeable in the afternoon, especially on my cheeks. My nose gets oily too, so I'm not sure whether I need cleansing.

Consultant: Let me make sure I've understood: what you most want to address is how your makeup looks in the afternoon, rather than simply getting your face very clean. Is that right?

Customer: Yes. I wouldn't want cleansing that leaves it even drier.

Consultant: Have you changed any products recently? Do you have any discomfort now, or have you had any other procedures recently? Those things affect whether today's trial is appropriate.

Customer: No other procedures. I just changed my cleanser, but I can't remember its name. There's no stinging, just a little tightness after washing.

Consultant: I'll record tightness after washing as your own experience. We don't have the product details, so I won't guess which ingredient might be responsible or rush to tell you to replace everything you use at home.

Customer: Can your device tell what my skin is lacking? Someone once showed me red and green images and said my problems were serious.

Consultant: Today's photos are mainly for records and to help us observe together, not for diagnosis. Lighting and whether you're wearing makeup can affect them. If you'd rather not take photos, we can keep written notes only.

Customer: Photos are okay, but don't use them for promotion.

Consultant: Understood, only for this service record. This photo shows your cheeks, and this one the area around your nose. Rather than draw a conclusion for you, which area matters most to you?

Customer: Still my cheeks. I don't mind a little oil on my nose; I can touch up my makeup.

Consultant: Then today's focus will be the basic-care trial and how comfortable you feel afterward. We can note the nose concern without adding every possible service today.

Customer: I bought a very expensive serum before because a friend liked it, but I didn't find it particularly good. So showing me other people's photos won't necessarily convince me.

Consultant: That's a fair concern. Someone else's satisfaction doesn't mean you will be satisfied too. We can compare your own experience, such as tightness afterward and how makeup feels later, instead of promising results based on someone else's face.

Customer: How many visits will it take to get better?

Consultant: I can't give you a guaranteed number now. The first visit is to check the service process and your experience, then see whether you want to continue. Today's photos can't prove what will happen in the future either.

Customer: Okay, tell me the price first. I don't want a long explanation only to find out it's very expensive.

Consultant: The trial you already bought is CNY 79 for one visit, including basic cleansing and moisturizing care, about 35 minutes, with no additional charge today. A regular single visit is CNY 240 for the same service, about 40 minutes. A four-visit package is CNY 880, valid for four months. More visits do not necessarily mean a better fit for you.

Customer: Do you secretly switch products between the trial and the regular service?

Consultant: All three options cover the same basic service; the trial is a first-visit price. The products actually used will be listed on the service form. We won't reduce the service you've bought because you don't buy a package.

Customer: Four visits for 880 is 220 each. That's 20 less than a single visit, but I don't know whether I'd use them all.

Consultant: Correct. Four individual visits cost 960, so the package saves 80 in total. That difference isn't a reason to overlook the time needed. If you're unsure how often you can come, single visits are more flexible.

Customer: I get busy and sometimes can't even come once a month. Could I buy products to use at home instead?

Consultant: In-salon services and home use can't simply be treated as equivalent. Let's finish today's trial first. If you want to learn about products later, we can review their instructions and prices separately; they don't need to be tied to a package.

Customer: I understand. I won't buy anything else today; I'll just use the trial.

Consultant: All right. Let me confirm: today is your prepaid CNY 79 trial, with no new spending, and we won't exchange your trial voucher for something else.

Customer: Right. I'll see how I feel afterward.

Consultant: Tell us at any point if you feel discomfort, and we'll stop. I'll also record the tightness after washing and afternoon makeup patchiness separately rather than assume they have the same cause.

Customer: You won't contact me every day afterward, will you?

Consultant: We'll follow your preferred arrangement. If you agree, we can ask about your experience once at a time you approve. Declining follow-up won't affect today's service.

Customer: Then a text every few days is fine. Don't call during work hours.

Consultant: Okay, we'll contact you only as agreed. If you choose to continue later, we'll review this experience first before discussing whether another visit is needed.

Customer: Fine, that's all for today.

Consultant: I'll show you the service form. We'll start after you confirm it.
