# Beauty Salon Sales Dialogue 04

> Fictional adaptation for training, not a real salon recording. People, services, prices, and transaction arrangements are fictional and are provided only for agent development practice. The dialogue has been edited and does not constitute medical or skincare advice.

Customer: I understand what the care includes, but I'd still like to go home and think about it.

Consultant: Of course. If you're willing, may I check whether your concern is the cost, the time, or the service itself? You can also simply go home and think; you don't need to answer now.

Customer: Mainly the price. I originally just wanted a trial, and suddenly you're talking about over 800. That feels like a big jump.

Consultant: I understand. A single trial and a package are very different outlays. I introduced the four-visit package too quickly; let's return to what you actually need today.

Customer: I just want occasional care to feel comfortable. I don't want to come to a salon every day.

Consultant: Then you don't need to buy on the assumption of a long-term schedule. Today's trial is basic cleansing and moisturizing care, and you can finish without buying a package. Are you trying to limit additional spending today or your total budget over the coming months?

Customer: Both. I didn't plan to spend much today, and I don't want endless extras later.

Consultant: All right, I'll write down two options. A regular single visit is CNY 240, paid per visit. Four visits cost CNY 880, valid for four months, with CNY 220 deducted each time. Both include basic care only, with no mandatory additional services.

Customer: Four visits would be 960, and now it's 880. That's only 80 less. Can you make it cheaper?

Consultant: Your calculation is right: the total saving is CNY 80. Those are the two prices on the current list, and I don't have a further discretionary discount. I also don't want to inflate the price first and then claim a special deal just for you.

Customer: Could you give me something free? Other salons do.

Consultant: If there is a published promotion, we can check its rules. There is no gift promotion being offered today. I can't invent a gift to close a sale, or add its cost back in at the end.

Customer: If I give you a positive review or bring a friend, can you lower the price?

Consultant: Your review should reflect your real experience, without being exchanged for today's price. Referring a friend is also your choice later; it isn't tied to this purchase.

Customer: So you don't have much in the way of discounts.

Consultant: There aren't many, that's true. We can come back to whether it's worth buying. If you're uncertain about continuing, not prepaying may suit you better than getting a discount you aren't sure you'll use.

Customer: It's not that I don't want it at all. Today felt comfortable. I'm afraid that after buying, you'll keep asking me to add things.

Consultant: That's the bigger concern, then. We'll put the basic service, total price, deduction per visit, and handling of unused visits in writing. Other services are quoted separately and won't be added without your confirmation.

Customer: Do the photos you mentioned cost extra too?

Consultant: No extra charge, and they aren't mandatory. They are service records, not a guarantee of results. If you don't want photos, we can use written notes only.

Customer: If I buy four visits but don't want to return after one, what happens?

Consultant: Under the order, one visit deducts CNY 220 from 880, leaving CNY 660 for three unused visits, which you can request back. That covers unused visits; it isn't a promise to refund all used service fees if you're dissatisfied. Any dispute over a used service will be handled based on the circumstances and order terms.

Customer: Good to make that clear. I was afraid "refundable" would later turn into nothing being refundable.

Consultant: If the terms you see differ from what I've said, we need to resolve that first. Proceed only with an order whose clarified terms you accept. Don't pay while anything remains unclear.

Customer: Can I pay 240 first, then pay the difference to convert it to a package later?

Consultant: There is currently no retroactive conversion rule, so I can't promise you'll be able to do that later. Choose between the two options based on the amount you're willing to commit now.

Customer: Let me think. I should be able to use four visits over the next few months. I just don't want to be pushed to come in.

Consultant: We can look at your general schedule, but don't squeeze in visits just to use the package. You need to be comfortable with the four-month validity. Each appointment will still be confirmed separately; we won't automatically book all of them.

Customer: Will it renew automatically after the four visits?

Consultant: No. After the visits, we'll first ask about your experience. If you want to continue, that is a separate purchase. The CNY 880 you pay now covers only four visits.

Customer: Today's trial is already finished. Will that count as one of the four?

Consultant: No. Today's completed CNY 79 trial is a separate order. If you buy the package, all four visits are new future services, and today's additional payment is CNY 880.

Customer: Okay, I accept that. Put the refund terms and no add-ons in writing.

Consultant: They're in the order; please read it first. It lists four basic-care visits, CNY 880 total, CNY 220 each, four-month validity, and unused balance calculated by remaining visits. No added products and no automatic renewal.

Customer: Yes, I see it. I'll book the first visit myself. Don't book it for me yet.

Consultant: Understood. Please confirm the order before paying, and we'll give you a copy of the details afterward.

Customer: Fine. I'll write a review based on how I actually felt.

Consultant: Of course. You can honestly describe both what you liked and anything you were unhappy with.
