# Beauty Salon Sales Dialogue 02

> Fictional adaptation for training, not a real salon recording. People, services, prices, and transaction arrangements are fictional and are provided only for agent development practice. The dialogue has been edited and does not constitute medical or skincare advice.

Consultant: You came for a trial last time. Would you like to discuss how it felt first, or is there a service you already want to book?

Customer: I felt comfortable right afterward, and the next day was okay too. But I went somewhere else for a while before, and it always felt good immediately, then went back to much the same. I don't want to spend more just for reassurance.

Consultant: You're concerned that it feels good at the time, but later it's hard to tell whether it was worth it. May I first ask what you liked about the previous service and what didn't meet your expectations?

Customer: They were friendly, and the massage felt good. But every time they recommended something new and said one thing had to go with another. When I asked what had actually changed, they showed me other people's pictures.

Consultant: So you're not opposed to care and you appreciated the service. What makes you hesitate is the lack of your own records and the rising cost. Is that right?

Customer: Yes. Are you the same? I don't want to hear you criticize them.

Consultant: We haven't seen their service, so we can't judge it for you. What I can explain is how we will keep records, charge, and confirm next steps today. You can compare those points.

Customer: Go ahead.

Consultant: One specific question first: what has been bothering you most about your face lately? If you could choose only one thing, what would you most like to focus on?

Customer: Tightness after washing and makeup not sitting well in the afternoon. I don't like my pores either, but they've been like that for years; I don't expect them to disappear overnight.

Consultant: Then let's first record the sensations after washing and how your makeup sits, since those matter most. You care about the appearance of your pores, but that doesn't have to be solved today, and we won't use it as a reason to add services.

Customer: That sounds more realistic. How would you keep records?

Consultant: Today we'll start with your own description, when it's most noticeable, and your recent routine. If you consent to photos, we'll try to use the same lighting and angles each time. Written records are also useful without photos.

Customer: I'm wearing makeup today, but I wasn't last time. Can those photos be compared?

Consultant: They can't directly prove a change. Today's photos can be kept as records, but that difference must be noted. We also can't turn your feeling comfortable into a claim that a particular skin issue has improved.

Customer: So what exactly am I paying for?

Consultant: A clearly defined basic-care service, plus the recording and communication we agree on during the process. We don't sell a guaranteed skin condition. If you want a definite treatment outcome, this service cannot meet that expectation.

Customer: My expectations aren't that high. I just don't want to be pushed into buying more and more.

Consultant: Then the decision to continue each time stays with you. There is no automatic renewal, and future add-on services aren't assumed. Anything extra today will be explained separately first; if you don't agree, we won't do it.

Customer: I saw your four-visit package. Is that a little cheaper?

Consultant: A regular single visit is CNY 240, and four visits are CNY 880, valid for four months. All four visits are the same basic care, not progressively stronger services, and there is no requirement to buy home-care products.

Customer: How often would I have to come for those four visits? I can't keep running here every day.

Consultant: We won't require you to come every day. First we'll check for discomfort and whether the service is suitable. Later bookings follow the service instructions and your circumstances. A schedule is an arrangement, not a promise of results; there's no need to pack visits together just to use them up.

Customer: I only know I'm free this week. Later is uncertain. What if I buy it and don't want to continue after two visits?

Consultant: Under these written salon rules, each visit deducts CNY 220 from the CNY 880 package. Unused visits can be refunded at CNY 220 each, without repricing used visits at the higher single-visit rate. After two visits, the remaining refundable amount is CNY 440. We'll give you a copy of the rules when you purchase.

Customer: You can say that now, but what if someone else refuses to honor it later?

Consultant: That's why my word alone isn't enough. The term must be written into the order you confirm. If it's missing from the actual order, don't pay yet; let us check the details first.

Customer: I'd still rather do one visit first to make sure you don't add charges at the last minute.

Consultant: That's fine. Today's single visit is CNY 240. Your previous trial is complete and won't be charged again. We also won't automatically turn today's payment into a package.

Customer: If I buy a four-visit package later, can today's visit count toward it?

Consultant: The current price rules don't provide for retroactive conversion after a visit, so I can't promise that. Today we'll follow the single visit you confirm. You don't need to buy early for a discount that might not exist.

Customer: Okay. Keep the photos only for me to review.

Consultant: Of course, we'll keep records only within the scope you agree to. Next time we'll first ask about your actual experience and anything you disliked, then discuss what comes next rather than starting with a new service recommendation.

Customer: Fine, just one visit today.

Consultant: The service form lists one basic facial-care visit at CNY 240, with no extras. Please confirm it before paying.
