# Beauty Salon Sales Dialogue 05

> Fictional adaptation for training, not a real salon recording. People, services, prices, and transaction arrangements are fictional and are provided only for agent development practice. The dialogue has been edited and does not constitute medical or skincare advice.

Consultant: You mentioned wanting to learn about facial care. What change would you most like to see?

Customer: I want to get rid of all these spots and lines. Some ads make people look much younger after a few visits. Can you do that?

Consultant: If you mean eliminating all the spots and lines, our basic care cannot promise that. Let's clarify expectations first, so you don't spend money and then discover you bought something different from what you wanted.

Customer: Then what is your care for? There has to be some change, surely.

Consultant: We offer a basic cleansing and moisturizing care experience. We can explain the process, products, and fees clearly, but we can't call it treatment or guarantee a particular change in your appearance.

Customer: I've been elsewhere, and they said things could improve gradually. Maybe I just haven't had enough visits?

Consultant: Without complete records, I can't judge what was done before or simply say there weren't enough visits. The time and money you've already spent don't mean you must keep spending now.

Customer: Look at my face first. I put something on today, so it might be hard to see.

Consultant: First, do you have any discomfort today, and have you had any other procedures recently? Makeup and lighting affect photos, and I won't use a photo to identify a specific condition.

Customer: No recent procedures or discomfort. I just don't like how it looks and want a noticeable change.

Consultant: Understood. Wanting a noticeable change and having discomfort today are separate issues. The fact that you aren't uncomfortable doesn't let us promise that basic care will address the spots and lines you care about.

Customer: Do you have something stronger? I could pay more.

Consultant: Our salon only offers non-invasive basic facial care, not injections, lasers, or treatment procedures. If you want to explore those needs, you can consult an appropriately qualified professional provider for an assessment. I can't choose such procedures for you here.

Customer: Okay, I don't want those right now either. If it's just about feeling more comfortable afterward, I'm fine with that. Do I have to buy a big package first?

Consultant: No. Let's confirm your revised goal: you want to try basic care today and accept that it does not promise to remove spots or lines. Is that right? If you still only want that result, this service would not be an appropriate purchase today.

Customer: Right. I understand you can't guarantee it. I'm happy to try it first.

Consultant: All right. The first-visit trial is CNY 79. After that, a regular single visit is CNY 240, or CNY 880 for four visits, valid for four months. You don't need to buy products to take today's trial.

Customer: Is the trial very different from regular care? I'm worried the cheap version is just a token service and you'll later tell me I have to pay more.

Consultant: The basic content is the same; the trial is a first-visit price. We'll provide what you purchased as listed on the service form. We won't suddenly say you must pay extra to complete it.

Customer: Tell me what a year would cost too. I don't want to look only at the low starting price.

Consultant: We can calculate a budget, but it will be based on a number of visits you assume, not a recommendation that you must come that often. What frequency would you like to use for the estimate?

Customer: Suppose twice a month, so 24 visits a year?

Consultant: At the regular single-visit rate, 24 × 240 is CNY 5760. Buying a CNY 880 package for every four visits would mean six packages for 24 visits, totaling CNY 5280. This comparison excludes the first-visit trial and any extra products or services.

Customer: So the difference is 480. Don't tell me later I have to add this and that.

Consultant: Correct, the difference is CNY 480. The arithmetic is certain; the number of visits you actually need is not. We won't turn this budget into an annual service recommendation, and we don't suggest paying for a whole year today.

Customer: If I only wanted to come once a month, would the cost be half?

Consultant: Assuming unchanged prices, the same service, and half as many visits, 12 single visits cost CNY 2880, or three four-visit packages cost CNY 2640. Each package still has a four-month validity period, so you must consider whether you can use it in time. If prices change later, they need confirmation before purchase; today's estimate doesn't lock in a year's prices.

Customer: That gives me an idea. If I don't buy a package after the trial, will you keep trying to persuade me?

Consultant: You've said you want to try it first, so we'll follow that choice. You can tell us how it felt afterward without deciding on long-term spending on the spot.

Customer: If I don't see much change after one visit, does that mean I need to keep going?

Consultant: We shouldn't respond to a lack of change by telling you to add visits. First consider what you expected, whether there's any discomfort, and whether the service fits your needs. In particular, we can't answer your original concerns about spots and lines with "keep going and it will improve."

Customer: Good. Please don't promise me that again. Guarantees were what tempted me before.

Consultant: Let me repeat today's service and cost: one basic-care trial at CNY 79, no new products, no long-term purchase requirement, and no promise to remove spots or lines.

Customer: Fine. I'll do that today, with no package.

Consultant: Understood. Tell us immediately if anything feels uncomfortable during the service, and we'll pause. We'll clarify any other issues first rather than expect you to push through just because you've paid.

Customer: Okay. If I want to come back, I'll book it myself. No need to contact me.

Consultant: Noted, no follow-up contact. Please keep a copy of today's service details.
