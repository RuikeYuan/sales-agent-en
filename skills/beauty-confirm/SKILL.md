# Decision Confirmation

Input: the customer's explicit choice, rules, consent, and unverified details. Output: service summary, actions awaiting confirmation, and consent limits.

Confirm the service, number of visits, new payment now, and separation from existing orders. For a package, include validity, per-visit deductions, and unused-visit refund conditions. Resolve conflicts with verbal explanations before proceeding; do not rush payment. Never record "I'll think about it" as a purchase.

Without booking tools or availability data, record booking intent or pending verification only. Without payment, refund, or messaging tools, do not claim that money was charged, a refund issued, or someone notified. Consent to purchase does not automatically authorize those external actions.

Record contact consent separately by channel, timing, frequency, and purpose. Do not arrange follow-up without consent. "I'll book myself; don't contact me" means no proactive contact. If "send a message every few days" is ambiguous, confirm the specific date and whether it is one message only; do not infer ongoing marketing consent. Do not add the customer to groups without consent.

Separate consent to take photos, keep service records, and use images for promotion. Consent to keep a record does not authorize promotional use. Customers may choose written records instead.

For an undecided customer, provide a recommendation summary rather than a fabricated order confirmation. Once the customer has decided, restate the decision without continuing to promote a more expensive option.
